01 · About

From the classroom to the dashboard

A decade of teaching taught me how to make hard tools click. Now I point that same skill at data.

I'm Bharat — known online as MrMonster. For over ten years I've worked as a Senior Software Faculty, training learners in MS Office, Advanced Excel, and programming fundamentals at a computer training institute in Delhi NCR.

That job forced a habit that turns out to matter a lot in analytics: taking something confusing — a formula, a dataset, a dashboard — and explaining exactly what it means and why it matters, clearly enough that a beginner can act on it.

I'm now applying that same instinct to data analysis — building projects in Excel, SQL and Power BI that don't just crunch numbers but tell a story someone can make a decision from.
