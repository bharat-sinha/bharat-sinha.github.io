Swiggy Sales & Customer Behavior Analysis

Order, revenue and regional demand dashboard (2026)

Problem
Understand revenue, demand trends and regional performance across ~197K orders (Rs. 53M+).
Approach
Cleaned and modelled data with Power Query and Pivot Tables, then built city/state dashboards with dynamic slicers benchmarking Average Order Value (Rs. 268).

Hospital Emergency Room Operations Analysis

ER wait time, satisfaction and load dashboard (2026)

Problem
Understand how wait times, patient demographics and referrals affect satisfaction across 9,216 ER visits.
